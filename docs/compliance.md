# Evidence checks and their limits

Sources checked 7 October 2026. /compliance checks recorded evidence. It does not establish legal compliance or certify that a person may work with children.

- Privacy retention: NZ Privacy Act principle 9 requires retention only while needed for a lawful purpose. Missing or past privacy_review_on flags a coordinator review. The law sets no universal review interval here; the church chooses the date. Nothing automatically deletes. [Source](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/).
- Data minimisation: principle 1 supports collecting only necessary information. Follow-ups require a purpose, and the importer intentionally keeps only identity and email. An operator still assesses necessity. [Source](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/1/).
- Security: principle 5 calls for reasonable safeguards. Database row security denies unprivileged access by default; the owner connection is for a trusted coordinator. This cannot test disk encryption, backups or who can open a report. Verify them before real data. [Source](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/).
- Screening: requires_screening is a church policy switch, not a statement of a universal NZ or Australian legal rule. Assignment requires a recorded evidence reference and a review date covering the service. /screening-due lists reviews in the next thirty days. Dates, jurisdiction, roles and evidence must be set by the church safeguarding lead. A reference is not a background check. [Planning Center scheduling context](https://www.planningcenter.com/services).
- Music: the permission flag is an internal evidence reminder, not a legal determination or licence purchase. A song reference and review date support /music-review. Store metadata only; this build supplies no protected lyrics, recordings or sheet music. The coordinator checks actual rights for each use.

Children's attendance, guardian authority, pickup matching and emergency care are outside this base. Do not use the roster as a child-release system. Giving, payment processing, tax receipts and bank feeds are also outside scope.
